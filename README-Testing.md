# Control Freak // Test Plan

Control Freak calls out taunts, interrupts, fears, loss of control, tank deaths and pet or priest mistakes while the fight is still going. This time we most want you to find your way around the reorganized options, where every list of abilities and every Tanking Tool has a page of its own, and to tank real groups with it on.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery and TBC Anniversary.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. Type `/freak`: in the list, **Taunts**, **Fears** and **Bad Pets** each have a **Tracked Abilities** page under them, and **Tanking Tools** has one page each for **Cold Openers**, **Armor Debuffs**, **Parries** and **Novas**.
2. On Control Freak's main page, untick **Enable Control Freak** and the feature switches below it hide; Left-Click the mini-map button and they come back.
3. Right-Click the mini-map button to turn Tanking Tools on and off: a **Tanking Tools** section with one switch per tool shows and hides on the main page, and each switch matches the one on that tool's own page.
4. Open **Feedback & Support**, click the **Discord** box, press Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C, and paste into your chat box: the whole link comes through.
5. On TBC Anniversary, have someone in your group use **Drums of Panic** near a pack: **AOE Fear!** prints.
6. While you tank, have a hunter leave **Growl** or a warlock leave their felguard's **Anguish** on auto-cast (felguards are on TBC Anniversary and Season of Discovery): **Bad Pet!** prints and the owner gets one whisper.
7. On **Diagnostic Tools**, tick **Enable Diagnostic Tools** and run the big button on each of the **Settings**, **Code**, **Data** and **Localization** tabs: every report, the new **Items** one included, fills in without an error.

## Everything Else

8. Log in with Control Freak as your only add-on: a welcome line prints, no error appears, and every sound in a **Play Sound** picker plays when you pick it.
9. While tanking, taunt an elite before it hits you (on Season of Discovery, try a rune taunt such as **Hand of Reckoning** or **Tease** too): **Taunt!** prints with a clickable spell and no full stop before **// Control Freak**, and a resisted taunt sends **Taunt Failed!** to party chat.
10. As a warrior in a group, use **Challenging Shout** and then **Intimidating Shout** on a pack: one party-chat line each, not one per mob.
11. Interrupt a casting mob: the **Interrupt!** line names the spell you stopped (check on Classic Era).
12. While you tank at high health, have a priest cast **Power Word: Shield** on you: **Bad Shield!** prints.
13. Die while tanking in a party, and again in a raid once you're set as Main Tank: **Tank Down!** prints with a sound each time.
14. Type `/freak`, then Shift + Middle-Click the mini-map button: each opens Control Freak inside the game's Options window, and in combat each refuses with a message instead.
