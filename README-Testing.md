# Control Freak // Test Plan

Control Freak calls out taunts, interrupts, fears, loss of control, tank deaths, classmates' cooldowns and other players' mistakes while the fight is still going. This time we most want you to try the new **Naughty List** tabs, **Bad Mages** and **Bad Parry Monkeys**, and the new **Print (Self Only)** and **Announce** controls on every alert.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery and TBC Anniversary.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. Open Control Freak's settings and walk the new **Monitoring**, **Naughty List** and **Tanking Tools** pages down to a **Tracked Abilities** page, such as the one under **Taunts**: every page opens inside the game's Options window, on TBC Anniversary too.
2. On Control Freak's main page, untick **Naughty List**: the four switches under it grey out, and ticking it again brings back exactly what was on.
3. On a warrior, switch **My Successful Taunts**' **Announce** between **Never** and **In a Party** on the **Taunts** page: the **Example:** line moves between your own window and party chat.
4. Set **My Successful Interrupts** to **In a Party**, then interrupt a casting mob in a dungeon group and again in a raid: the **Interrupt!** line names the spell you stopped, goes to party chat in the dungeon, and only prints for you in the raid.
5. In a party where someone has the Tank role selected, have a mage cast **Frost Nova**: **AOE Nova!** prints.
6. In a raid with a Main Tank set, melee a raid boss from the front while someone else tanks it until it parries you: **I'm a naughty parry monkey! (x1)** goes to raid chat, and later lines, at most one every 30 seconds, show the count climbing.
7. On Classic Era or TBC Anniversary, as a priest, shield a warrior or druid tank who runs Control Freak, has the Tank role selected in a party, and is mid-fight at high health: you get a whisper starting **Naughty Priest!**, and a shield before the pull gets none.
8. As a warrior in a party with nobody on the Tank role, use **Challenging Shout** away from any mob and **Intimidating Shout** on a mob: each still goes to party chat, whatever it hits.
9. Hover the mini-map button: Left-Click turns **Monitoring** on and off, Right-Click **Naughty List**, and Shift + Left-Click **Tanking Tools**, and with **Enable Control Freak** unticked the tooltip says nothing fires.
10. If you played the last release, log in with your old settings: lines you had announcing still announce, and Parries you had on with **Tanking Tools** switched on are now on under **Bad Parry Monkeys**.

## Everything Else

11. Log in with Control Freak as your only add-on: a welcome line prints, no error appears, and every sound in a **Play Sound** picker except **None** plays when you pick it.
12. While tanking in a party with the Tank role selected, taunt an elite before it hits you (on Season of Discovery, try a rune taunt such as **Hand of Reckoning** or **Tease** too): **Taunt!** prints with a clickable spell, and a resisted taunt sends **Taunt Failed!** to party chat.
13. Die with the Tank role selected in a party, and again in a raid once you're set as Main Tank: **Tank Down!** prints with a sound each time.
14. Type `/freak`, then Shift + Middle-Click the mini-map button: each opens Control Freak inside the game's Options window, and in combat each refuses with a message instead.
