# Control Freak // Test Plan

Control Freak calls out taunts, interrupts, fears, loss of control, tank deaths, pet or priest mistakes and, new this time, your classmates' cooldowns while the fight is still going. This time we most want you to try **Peer Pressure** in groups with other players of your class, and to keep tanking real groups with Control Freak on.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery and TBC Anniversary.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. In a group with another player of your class, have them use a ticked cooldown from **Peer Pressure**'s **Tracked Abilities** page, such as **Blade Flurry** or **Shield Wall**: **Peer Pressure!** prints with a thunderclap.
2. Tick **My Cooldowns** on the **Peer Pressure** page and use one of your own ticked cooldowns: it prints for you too.
3. On Control Freak's main page, **Enable Control Freak** sits under its own **Master Switch** header, and every switch under **Features** has an icon, **Peer Pressure** included.
4. Untick **Enable Control Freak**, then open **Taunts** and its **Tracked Abilities** page: each says at the top that nothing on it fires (tick it back on afterwards).
5. On a warrior, switch **My Successful Taunts** on the **Taunts** page between **Print (Self Only)** and **Announce**: the **Example:** line under that section changes to match, with your character's name and a Molten Core or Blackwing Lair boss.
6. On the **Tracked Abilities** page under **Taunts**, each taunt shows **Single Target** or **AOE** as plain text, and only **Righteous Defense** on TBC Anniversary has a dropdown.
7. On Season of Discovery, open **Bad Priests**: a line says Season of Discovery never reports a bad shield.

## Everything Else

8. Log in with Control Freak as your only add-on: a welcome line prints, no error appears, and every sound in a **Play Sound** picker except **None** plays when you pick it.
9. While tanking with the Tank role selected, taunt an elite before it hits you (on Season of Discovery, try a rune taunt such as **Hand of Reckoning** or **Tease** too): **Taunt!** prints with a clickable spell and no full stop before **// Control Freak**, and a resisted taunt sends **Taunt Failed!** to party chat.
10. As a warrior in a group with the Tank role selected, use **Challenging Shout** and then **Intimidating Shout** on a pack: one party-chat line each, not one per mob.
11. Interrupt a casting mob: the **Interrupt!** line names the spell you stopped (check on Classic Era).
12. On Classic Era or TBC Anniversary, as a warrior or druid with the Tank role selected and at high health, have a priest cast **Power Word: Shield** on you: **Bad Shield!** prints.
13. Die with the Tank role selected in a party, and again in a raid once you're set as Main Tank: **Tank Down!** prints with a sound each time.
14. Type `/freak`, then Shift + Middle-Click the mini-map button: each opens Control Freak inside the game's Options window, and in combat each refuses with a message instead.
