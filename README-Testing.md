# Control Freak // Manual Test Plan

This is the manual test plan for Control Freak, the steps to confirm it works before a release is tagged. For what it does, see [README.md](https://github.com/Gogo1951/Control-Freak/blob/main/README.md); for how it works, see [README-Technical.md](https://github.com/Gogo1951/Control-Freak/blob/main/README-Technical.md).

## Before you start

**Run the whole list on Classic Era, then `/reload` and run it again on TBC Anniversary.** Steps are numbered continuously so you can report "failed on step N."

Gather these once so you aren't caught short mid-run:

- **This build installed on both clients**, in each client's own `Interface/AddOns` folder. The TBC Anniversary folder may still hold the 2026.09.12.A release, so check the version line at the bottom of the main Control Freak panel there: it must not read 2026.09.12.A.
- **A hunter or warlock of your own**, level 10 or higher, with its pet out and the pet's taunt (Growl or Torment) on auto-cast. Steps 1 to 4 run on that character, and none of them needs the Tank role.
- **A warrior of level 26 or higher with a shield equipped**, for step 5 onward. Taunt, Challenging Shout, Intimidating Shout and Shield Bash cover every combat step you run yourself, and a warrior is the kind of tank the Bad Priests tab watches.
- **A second player in your group** who swaps characters along the way: a warlock of level 24 or higher with a Voidwalker for steps 5 and 6, since that is the level the Voidwalker learns Suffering, its AOE taunt, and a priest for steps 20 and 21. Any class works for everything else, and the second player never holds the Tank role.
- **The Tank role, and the group's lead.** Pick the Tank role in the group finder and be the group's leader: steps 7 to 9 convert the group to a raid, set Main Tank and convert it back, which only a leader can do. In a raid only Main Tank counts and the group finder's role does nothing, and every line this plan expects in party chat arrives in raid chat instead. After each conversion and each relog, check you still hold the Tank role, and pick it again if the game cleared it.
- **Mobs to fight:** elites at or a few levels above your level, because the taunt alerts ship counting only elites and bosses and a higher level makes resists and parries easier to produce; a pack of three or more for the shouts; and a mob that casts spells, for the interrupt.
- **Control Freak as your only enabled add-on** for step 10. You can turn the others back on after it.
- **A non-English client**, only for the optional step 23.

Unless a step says otherwise, be **out of combat** in the open world, grouped with your second player as a party, and, from step 5 on, playing your warrior with the Tank role. The options panel does not open in combat, so every step changes its settings before a pull or after the fight. A taunt is sometimes resisted: when a step expects a **Taunt!** line and a **Taunt Failed!** line arrives in group chat instead, repeat that part on a fresh elite.

**What this plan deliberately skips:** Cold Openers, Armor Debuffs, Novas and **Ignore Tanks** on the Tanking Tools tab; the Bad Shields whisper, which ships off; two clients both running Control Freak sending one whisper between them; the **As a Healer** rung, and the Healer and class-name openings of the Incapacitated line; Incapacitated's nine effect boxes; the middle rungs of the target dropdown and **While in Instances**; the item rows (Target Dummies, which taunt, and Flash Bomb, which fears); and Season of Discovery's rune taunts.

## Verify this release's changes

These steps cover what changed since 2026.09.12.A, the last release.

**Shipped defaults**

**1.** Go to **Profiles** and click **Reset Profile**. The mini-map button jumping back to its default spot is expected. Open **Tanking Tools** first: **Enable Tanking Tools** must be **unticked**, the one feature tab that ships off, with only its dropdown beside it and nothing drawn below it. Tick it, and leave it ticked for the rest of this plan. Then read every feature tab. Exactly four rows may read **Announce**: **My Failed Taunts**, **My AOE Taunts**, **My Successful Fears** and **My Long Incapacitation**. Every other row with that dropdown must be ticked and read **Print (Self Only)**, **My Death** included. On **Tank Deaths**, **Play Sound** must be ticked on **Control Freak: Game Over**. On **Incapacitated**, **Play Sound** must be unticked, with its picker on **Control Freak: Negative Beeps**. Under **Parries** on Tanking Tools, **Ignore Tanks** and **Ignore Pets** must both be ticked, and **Whisper the Culprit** ticked at **9 Second Cooldown**. **Whisper the Pet Owner** on Bad Pets must be ticked at **1 Minute Cooldown**, **Whisper the Caster** on Bad Priests unticked, and **Cold Openers** must be the one unticked section on Tanking Tools, its rows hidden. Failure is **My Death** or any other fifth row on Announce, which means a fresh install talks in group chat unasked, a different sound on either tab, an unticked **Ignore Pets**, or Tanking Tools already ticked after the reset.

**Relabeled switches and new tooltips**

**2.** Read every feature tab again. Each alert section's switch must now open with **Enable Alerts for**, as in **Enable Alerts for Successful Interrupts On** on Interrupts. Each must show in full with no ellipsis, with its dropdown on the same line wherever it has one, and the word **Notifications** may not appear on any tab. On **Tanking Tools**, tick **Cold Openers** and hover **My Cold Openers**, then **My Parries** and **Others' Parries**. Each tooltip must say what that row reports (an opening ability that failed to land, a mob parrying your attacks, a mob parrying anybody else in your group), not the shared line about casts. Untick **Cold Openers** again. On **Fears**, the Example must read "Example: AOE Fear! Joe used Psychic Scream. // Control Freak", naming no mob and with no brackets around the spell. Open **Apology**: the letter must close on "Cheers!", then a gap, then **Gogo** and **September 2026** in gray. Failure is a switch still reading Notifications, a label cut short, a dropdown wrapped onto its own line, a row tooltip about casts, an Example that names a mob, or a letter with no gray signature under its sign-off.

**Mini-map button**

**3.** Hover the mini-map button. The tooltip must read, top to bottom: **Control Freak** with the version on the right; **All Alerts** / **Enabled**, "Turn every Control Freak alert on or off." and **Left-Click** / **Toggle**; **Tanking Tools** / **Enabled**, "Cold openers, armor debuffs, parries, and novas." on no more than two lines, and **Right-Click** / **Toggle**; and last **Control Freak Options**, with **Shift + Middle-Click** on the line under it. No other tab may appear. With the options panel open on **Tanking Tools**, Right-Click the button: **Enable Tanking Tools** must untick and the tooltip must read **Tanking Tools** / **Disabled**. Right-Click again to switch it back on, then open **Bad Pets**: **Enable Bad Pet Monitoring** must still be ticked. The button's icon must fill its round frame the way the icons on the game's own mini-map buttons do, with no dark square edge showing inside the ring. Failure is a Bad Pets block or any other tab in the tooltip, Right-Click switching anything but Tanking Tools, the tooltip and the panel disagreeing, or an icon that looks shrunken or clipped inside its ring.

**Bad Pets lines and whispers**

**4.** Still on your own hunter or warlock, open **Bad Pets** and untick **When Group Has a Tank** for this step. Send your pet at a mob with its taunt on auto-cast. Your window must print "Bad Pet! <your name>'s pet <pet> used [<taunt>] on <mob>. // Control Freak", naming **you** as the owner, and no whisper may go out. Once the fight is over, re-tick **When Group Has a Tank**, then log in on your warrior. Failure is a line opening "Bad Pet! Your pet", no line at all, or any whisper.

**5.** Have your second player switch to the warlock and turn **Torment**'s auto-cast off. Keep the Voidwalker alive until step 6 is done, because a resummoned one counts as a new pet. Have it cast **Suffering** beside a mob. Your window must print "Bad Pet! <owner>'s pet <pet> used [Suffering]. // Control Freak", naming no mob. About a second later the warlock must receive exactly **one** whisper: "Your pet <pet> used [Suffering]. Right-click the ability to turn off auto-cast. // Control Freak". Within the next minute, have the Voidwalker cast **Torment** on a mob: nothing may print and no second whisper may go out, because the **1 Minute Cooldown** covers that pet's line and whisper both. Once the minute is up, have it cast Torment again. Your window must print "Bad Pet! <owner>'s pet <pet> used [Torment] on <mob>. // Control Freak", and the whisper must read "Your pet <pet> used [Torment] on <mob>. Right-click the ability to turn off auto-cast. // Control Freak". Failure is no whisper after Suffering, a Suffering line or whisper naming a mob or "an unknown target", a line or whisper inside the minute, or a whisper sent to you.

**Parries: Ignore Pets**

**6.** On **Tanking Tools**, untick **Ignore Pets**. Put a skull on an elite, pull it, and hold it while it hits you. Have your second player, still on the warlock, stand beside you in front of it and melee it alongside the Voidwalker, with Torment's auto-cast still off, until your window has printed "Parry Haste! <their name> is standing in front of <skull icon> <mob>. // Control Freak" and the same line naming the Voidwalker. Each line must come with the **Control Freak: Parry** sound. Every line naming the warlock must bring them exactly **one** whisper, "Parry Haste! Please get behind <mob>: every parry speeds up its next swing. // Control Freak", and a line naming the Voidwalker must bring no whisper at all. A second parry on the same attacker inside 9 seconds must add nothing. Kill it, tick **Ignore Pets** again, and pull a second skulled elite the same way until two more lines have named the warlock and your second player's combat log has shown the Voidwalker parried at least once: no line may name the Voidwalker. Parries ships counting only bosses, so the skull is what lets these elites through. Failure is no line at all, a line naming the Voidwalker while **Ignore Pets** is ticked, a whisper addressed to the Voidwalker or to you, or a second line or whisper for one attacker inside those 9 seconds.

**Only Main Tank counts in a raid**

**7.** In the party, open **Diagnostic Tools**, tick **Enable Diagnostic Tools** and click **Read Alert Gate State**. The report must include `Tanks (outside a raid the group finder's Tank role counts): player (<your name>)`. Convert the group to a raid, keep your Tank role (pick it again if the conversion cleared it), and set nobody as Main Tank. Click the button again. The `taunts:` line must end in `IsFeatureGateOpen=false`, one line must begin `Tanks: none (in a raid only the Main Tank assignment counts`, and the line under it must read `Tank role tags ignored in a raid: player (<your name>)`. Taunt an elite that is not attacking you yet: **nothing** may print. Once that fight is over, set yourself as Main Tank and click the button again. The `Tanks` line must now list you, the ignored line must be gone, and the `taunts:` line must end in `IsFeatureGateOpen=true`. Taunt a fresh elite: the **Taunt!** line must print. Stay in the raid as Main Tank. Failure is a taunt alert while you hold only the Tank role in the raid, a report that still counts that role there, or silence once you are Main Tank.

**Tank Deaths**

**8.** Still Main Tank in the raid, die. Your window must print "Tank Down! <your name> has died. // Control Freak" once, with the **Control Freak: Game Over** sound, and nothing may go to raid chat, because **My Death** now ships on Print. Once you are back on your feet, remove your Main Tank assignment, keep the Tank role, and die again: **no** line may print, because in a raid the Tank role alone does not make you a tank. Failure is the line arriving in raid chat, a sound other than Game Over, no line while you are Main Tank, or a **Tank Down!** line without the assignment.

**9.** On **Tank Deaths**, tick your second player's class under **Deaths by Class**, then type `/reload` and reopen **Tank Deaths**: the box must still be ticked. With nobody set as Main Tank, have your second player die. Your window must print "<their class> Down! <their name> has died. // Control Freak", with no sound and no **Tank Down!** line. Set them as Main Tank and have them die again. Your window must print "Tank Down! <their name> has died. // Control Freak" with the **Control Freak: Game Over** sound, and **not** the class line as well. Remove their assignment, untick their class, and convert the group back to a party. Failure is a box that unticked itself across the reload, two lines for one death, a sound with the class line, either line in raid chat, or a **Tank Down!** line for a death without Main Tank.

When steps 1-9 pass on both flavors, this release's changes are verified. Proceed to `4 - Pre-Launch Review Prompt.md`.

## Core checks

**10.** Log out to the character select screen, open **AddOns**, disable every add-on except Control Freak, and log in. No Lua error window may appear, no red error text may print, and a colored welcome line must read "Control Freak // Version ..." and say the settings live under Options > AddOns > Control Freak. Type `/reload`: the same must be true again. Open the **Play Sound** picker under **Successful Taunts**. It must list **None** and eighteen Control Freak sounds, in alphabetical order: AOE Taunt, Defeat, Failure, Failure 2, Failure 3, Failure 4, Fear, Game Over, Interrupt, Magic, Magic 2, Negative Beeps, Nova, Parry, Sad Trumpet, Tank Death, Taunt, Taunt Resist. Pick each in turn: every one must play the moment you pick it. Finish on **Control Freak: Taunt**, untick **Play Sound**, and click the speaker: it must still play. Re-tick Play Sound. **Run this step with Control Freak alone:** another add-on that ships the same sound library can hide a broken one. Failure is an error naming Control Freak or LibSharedMedia, a line containing `nil` or a stray `%s`, no welcome line, a picker holding only None, or a sound that stays silent.

**11.** Open the options panel three ways: type `/freak`, the add-on's only slash command; **Shift + Middle-Click** the mini-map button; and press `Esc`, choose **Options**, then **AddOns**, and select **Control Freak**. All three must land **docked inside the Blizzard Options window**, with Control Freak selected in the category list on the left. Under it, the list must read **Taunts**, **Interrupts**, **Fears**, **Incapacitated**, **Tank Deaths**, **Bad Priests**, **Bad Pets**, **Tanking Tools**, **Profiles**, **Diagnostic Tools** and **Apology**, in that order. Failure looks like either nothing happening at all, or a standalone window floating free of the Options frame. **TBC Anniversary is the client where this panel has historically floated free, so a run on Classic Era alone has not finished this step.**

**12.** Pull a mob, and while still in combat type `/freak`, then Shift + Middle-Click the mini-map button. Each must print "Control Freak // As a safety precaution, the Options Interface cannot be opened during combat." and the panel must **not** open. Kill the mob and wait: the panel must not open by itself once combat ends. Failure is the panel opening, silence with no message, or a red `ADDON_ACTION_BLOCKED` error.

**13.** With the options panel open on the main **Control Freak** page, Shift + Left-Click and then Shift + Right-Click the mini-map button: neither may change anything on the panel or in the tooltip. Left-Click it. The tooltip must read **All Alerts** / **Disabled** with its Tanking Tools block gone, and **Enable Control Freak** under **All Alerts** must untick. Right-Click must do nothing while it is off. Taunt an elite that is not attacking you yet: **nothing** may print and no sound may play. Once the fight is over, Left-Click again: the tooltip must read **Tanking Tools** / **Enabled**, untouched by that Right-Click. Taunt a fresh elite: the **Taunt!** line must print. Failure is a Shift-click doing anything, an alert while **All Alerts** reads Disabled, Tanking Tools switched off by the Right-Click, or the tooltip and the panel disagreeing.

**14.** Taunt an elite that is not attacking you yet. Your window must print "Taunt! <your name> used [Taunt] on <mob>. // Control Freak", with your name in your class color, the spell as a **clickable link**, and the **Control Freak: Taunt** sound. Let it hit you until Taunt is ready again, then taunt it again: no **Taunt!** line may print, because a taunt on a mob already hitting you is a threat refresh rather than a save. Failure is no line on the pull, a **Taunt!** line on the second taunt, or the spell name as plain text. **Classic Era is the client that hands back a plain name where a link belongs, so check the link there.**

**15.** On **Taunts**, set the dropdown beside **Enable Alerts for Successful Taunts On** to **Bosses**, then taunt an unmarked elite that is not attacking you yet: no **Taunt!** line may print. Put a skull on a second elite and taunt it before it swings at you. Your window must print "Taunt! <your name> used [Taunt] on <skull icon> <mob>. // Control Freak", because a raid mark overrides the dropdown while **Always Alert on Marked Targets** is ticked. Once the fight is over, set the dropdown back to **Elites & Bosses**. Failure is a **Taunt!** line for the unmarked elite, or silence for the marked one.

**16.** Put a skull on an elite several levels above you, and taunt it until a taunt misses, is resisted, or hits something immune. The **Control Freak: Failure 4** sound must play, and one line must arrive as a **party-chat message from you**, as one complete sentence, such as "Taunt Failed! <your name>'s [Taunt] on <skull icon> <mob> was resisted. // Control Freak". An immune mob reads "Taunt Failed! <skull icon> <mob> is immune to <your name>'s [Taunt]. // Control Freak". The spell must be a working link, and the skull must show as its icon rather than as `{rt8}` text. Failure is a line that never arrives, a broken or half-rendered link, a second copy printed as a plain line, or a sound with no line anywhere to explain it.

**17.** Use **Challenging Shout** on a pack of three or more mobs, then **Intimidating Shout** on the same pack. Two lines must arrive, each exactly once, as **party-chat messages from you**: "AOE Taunt! <your name> used [Challenging Shout]. // Control Freak" and "AOE Fear! <your name> used [Intimidating Shout]. // Control Freak", because **My AOE Taunts** and **My Successful Fears** ship on Announce. Failure is a line repeated once per mob, a missing line after a shout that plainly landed, or a second copy printed as a plain line.

**18.** Interrupt a casting mob with **Shield Bash**. Your window must print "Interrupt! <your name>'s [Shield Bash] on <mob> stopped [<the spell it was casting>]. // Control Freak", with the stopped spell named. **Classic Era is the client that reports the stopped spell without an id, so check this there.** Failure is the end of the line reading "an unknown spell" or `[0]`.

**19.** On **Taunts**, read **AOE Taunt Abilities**, and on **Fears**, read **Fear Abilities**. The Paladin row **Righteous Defense** must appear under AOE Taunt Abilities **on TBC Anniversary and be absent on Classic Era**, and the Paladin row under Fear Abilities must read **Turn Undead** on Classic Era and **Turn Evil** on TBC Anniversary, a renamed ability rather than a broken one. Hover any row: the game's own spell tooltip must appear. Untick **Taunt** under Taunt Abilities and taunt an elite that is not attacking you yet: **no** alert may fire, not even a **Taunt Failed!** line. Once the fight is over, re-tick it, and check it is ticked before you go on. Failure is a row for an ability this client does not have, a row labelled with a number or a blank, or an alert from an unticked ability.

**20.** Have your second player, on the priest, duel you and cast **Psychic Scream** on you. One line must arrive as a **party-chat message from you**, reading "Tank Incapacitated for <seconds> seconds; <your name> is afflicted by [Psychic Scream] (Magic) from <priest>. // Control Freak", and Control Freak must not also print it as a plain line. An effect lasting at least the **4 Seconds** set under **Minimum for "Long" Incapacitation** answers to **My Long Incapacitation**, which ships on Announce. The Fears tab printing its own "AOE Fear! <priest> used [Psychic Scream]. // Control Freak" in your window is expected. Failure is no line, a printed copy of the Incapacitated line, a decimal in the seconds, or "an unknown caster" where the priest's name belongs.

**21.** With your health above 30%, have your second player, on the priest, cast **Power Word: Shield** on you. Your window must print "Bad Shield! <priest>'s [Power Word: Shield] on <your name>. // Control Freak", the **Control Freak: Magic 2** sound must play, and the priest must receive **no** whisper, because **Whisper the Caster** ships off. Failure is no line, no sound, or a whisper arriving.

**22.** Type `/reload` and open **Diagnostic Tools**. Only two things may show: the warning paragraph and **Enable Diagnostic Tools**, unticked, even though you ticked it in step 7. Tick it, and these sections must appear without reopening the panel: Event Log, Event Registration, API Endpoints, Alert Gate, Display Context, Validate Data: Data/Abilities.lua, Validate Data: Data/Data.lua, Other Add-ons, Saved Variables, Library Versions, Taint Log and External Tools. Failure is the box still ticked after the reload, report buttons showing before you tick it, or a missing section.

**23.** *Optional, and only worth running on a non-English client.* Log in on one, read the options panel and the mini-map tooltip, and set off one taunt line, one Incapacitated line and one Tank Deaths line. Every label, tooltip, description and Example line must render in that language, with no raw keys such as `TANKING_TOOLS_MINIMAP_SUMMARY` where words belong. Each alert must read as one complete sentence with the name, spell, mob and seconds in sensible places: no `nil`, no stray `%s` or `%d`, and no value appearing twice. This release's new text is the likeliest to show up in English instead, so check it first: the Tanking Tools summary in the mini-map tooltip, **Ignore Pets** and its tooltip, and the tooltips on **My Cold Openers**, **My Parries** and **Others' Parries**. Three things are **not** failures: the **Diagnostic Tools** and **Apology** panels are deliberately English everywhere; a translation that reorders the sentence is intended as long as it is grammatical; and a long line that arrives in chat without its closing `// Control Freak` has had the name dropped on purpose to fit the chat limit.

When every step passes on both Classic Era and TBC Anniversary, manual testing is complete. Proceed to `4 - Pre-Launch Review Prompt.md`.
